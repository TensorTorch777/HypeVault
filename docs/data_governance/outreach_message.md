# Outreach message (template)

Send only from an accountable person at the project. Replace every bracketed field. Do not imply that HypeVault is a certified authenticator or that any agreement already exists.

---

Subject: Research collaboration request — independently verified luxury-watch images

Dear [name],

I lead [project or institution]'s research on image-based screening of counterfeit luxury watches. Our current dataset does not have independent label provenance, so we cannot use it to make authenticity claims. We are looking for a partner who can examine physical watches and record why each one is authentic or counterfeit.

What we would ask for:

- Photographs of watches you have examined, taken under a capture protocol we would agree together.
- For each watch: your conclusion (authentic or counterfeit), how you reached it, and an evidence reference such as a service record, movement inspection, or case-back serial check.
- Written permission covering how the images may be used for research, how long we may keep them, and whether any image may be shown publicly.

What we would commit to:

- Use the images only for the agreed research purpose. We would not resell them or use them for marketing.
- Store them access-controlled. We would record your identity only as a private reference and not publish it unless you ask us to.
- Share results with you before publication, and credit you as you prefer.
- Delete the images on request, within the agreed retention terms.

We have not yet built a model on verified data, and we will not describe any result as an authenticity certificate.

Would you be open to a short call? I can share our draft data-contribution terms, which are still subject to legal review, and the evidence form we would ask you to complete.

Kind regards,
[name, role, contact]
