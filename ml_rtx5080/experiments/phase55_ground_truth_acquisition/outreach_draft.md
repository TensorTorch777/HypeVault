**Subject:** Research collaboration inquiry — independently verified watch labels and image-use rights

Hello,

I am working on HypeVault, a research project evaluating image-based watch-screening models. We are assessing whether a small, carefully governed feasibility pilot could be supported by independently verified physical-watch outcomes and image rights that explicitly permit non-public machine-learning evaluation.

We are not asking you to endorse an AI model or to provide any customer data at this stage. Before any photographs, certificates, confidential information, or physical items are exchanged, could you advise whether you offer a research or bulk collaboration covering both genuine and counterfeit outcomes?

We would need to understand:

1. Who conducts the examination, their relevant qualifications and brand/reference scope, and how conflicts of interest are controlled.
2. Whether each outcome can be `AUTHENTIC`, `COUNTERFEIT`, or `INDETERMINATE`, supported by a redacted written evidence report that is tied to the same physical watch.
3. Whether two qualified reviewers can work independently, blinded to model predictions and each other's decisions, with a separate adjudicator for disagreement.
4. Whether multiple views of the same physical watch can be linked with a pseudonymous product identifier without exposing customer identity or raw serial numbers in benchmark artifacts.
5. Who holds the rights to the image material and whether a separate written license can expressly permit the intended non-public ML-evaluation/research use.
6. The allowed purpose, territory, duration, storage/access controls, confidentiality, retention/deletion, and redisclosure limitations. We would need clear terms that preserve the applicable rights-holder's ownership and do not transfer unrelated HypeVault IP.
7. Which brands and both outcome classes are in scope, the number of independent physical products potentially available, likely timelines and the full cost/quote.
8. Whether you can share a redacted sample evidence report and standard terms for review before any commitment or data exchange.

We would not request customer identities or publish unredacted serial numbers. No image would be used before written permission from the relevant rights-holder and review of the handling terms.

Please let us know whether this is feasible and, if so, the appropriate research or business contact.

Regards,  
HypeVault research team
