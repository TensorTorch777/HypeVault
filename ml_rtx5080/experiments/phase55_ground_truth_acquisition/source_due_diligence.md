## Decision rule

A usable primary-benchmark item needs both (a) defensible evidence tied to the same physical watch and (b) written rights permitting the exact intended research/evaluation use of the image. An online listing, directory label, sale record, visual impression, AI-model output, or agreement between AI models is not sufficient ground truth.

## Minimum qualification questions

1. Who physically examines the watch, what qualifications and brand/reference scope support the outcome, and what conflicts of interest exist?
2. Can the examiner return `AUTHENTIC`, `COUNTERFEIT`, and `INDETERMINATE` with a redacted evidence report tied to one physical product?
3. Can two qualified reviewers work independently and blind to each other's outcome and model predictions, with adjudication of disagreements?
4. Can the source identify a physical watch across multiple images without putting serial numbers or customer identity into committed artifacts?
5. Who owns the images, and what rights-holder grants permission for storing and using the exact images for non-public ML evaluation/research?
6. Do written terms explicitly cover ML evaluation, geography, duration, access, retention, deletion, redisclosure, privacy and confidentiality?
7. Which brands and both outcome classes can be supplied, at what independent product counts, cost and lead time?
8. Can the party provide a redacted sample report and written terms before any data exchange?

## Sourcing safeguards

- Do not handle, buy, transport, or solicit suspected counterfeit goods outside a documented lawful process with qualified custody/examination and legal/compliance review.
- Do not ingest scraped watch images, auction images, marketplace images, screenshots, certificates, or private customer data without written rights and approved handling terms.
- A certificate/public web page is evidence only when tied to the same physical product and the relevant rights and limitations are documented.
- Keep `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_RECORDED`, `INDETERMINATE`, and denied permissions explicit. Do not fill gaps through visual guesses.
- Any acquisition pilot needs a separate explicit approval after source, rights, privacy, cost and storage controls are reviewed.
