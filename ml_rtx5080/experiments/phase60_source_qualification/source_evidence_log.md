Evidence classes used below:

- **Self-published:** the provider's own marketing or service page.
- **Official:** a brand or auction-house page or document describing its own programme or terms.
- **Independently confirmed:** a record from a party other than the claimant that verifies the operational claim. None of the rows below has this class.
